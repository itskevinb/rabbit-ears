            Picture and sound

            Picture changes take effect within a second. Matching each show plays film at 24 fps and TV transfers at 30, so nothing gets frames repeated or dropped (the stutter); the highest frame rate still caps it. A 30 fps show sent at 24 blends neighbouring frames by default, so pans glide instead of jumping every fifth frame. Keep it at 24: at 30 the board falls behind (measured 88% of real time at 256x192), which comes out as sound gaps and frozen frames.

            Schedule

            Prime time runs from its hour until late night (set it to the late-night hour for none); daytime plays the rest. With more time to fill than the most commercials in a row, a short cartoon goes in first. Halloween and Christmas shows only play in their season, and more often then.
